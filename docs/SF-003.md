## Date origin, precision and source holds

The [upstream date correction](https://github.com/CIAT/ERA_dev/pull/1) fixes mixed
character/Date handling before source cohorts are combined. Valid typed dates
remain unchanged; legacy serial/text dates are decoded at their source. The
consumer applies no universal day offset. The corrected JSON SHA-256 is
`c02bace9352800d7f3c6c07303753efe36b2e5dce52728dc70ae03249da1f0cb`.
This is a date-only successor: source extraction versions, non-date values,
source row ordinals, vocabulary and compiled comparisons remain unchanged.

`planting_date_raw` preserves the corrected source planting text before the
consumer screen. Original pre-decoding tokens and statuses remain in the
bundled `issue94-date-audit.csv`; this raw field is not a replacement for that
audit. `planting_date` requires round-trip `YYYY-MM-DD`, a unique recorded
season year equal to the planting year, and a year other than 9999. Partial,
invalid or inconsistent dates become missing in the harmonised planting field.
No day is invented from a month or year; cross-year and multiple-cycle season
interpretation remain open. Harvest and transplanting fields retain source
text and are not certified season windows.

The bundled `issue94-date-holds.csv` contains **900 field findings, not 900
independent outcomes**: 584 two-digit-year bounds in DK0117, eight invalid
30 February 2011 bounds in EO0115, 22 month-only fertilizer dates in AG0029,
four partial residue dates in NJ0022, 256 early-year bounds in DK0117 and
26 year-9999 bounds in NN0549. The last two categories remain in the source
as flagged values; no century or universal source-sentinel interpretation is
adopted. The consumer excludes year 9999 from accepted planting dates.

Holds use `(table, merged_row, field)` in this exact JSON, with `B.Code` checked
against the source. `source_row` in the audit denotes the cohort row; it is
**merged_row** that matches the consumer’s ERA Data.Out `source_row`. Do not
join different tables on row number. The build verifies all hold locations
and retains the full ledger as the local RDS attribute `era_date_holds`, with
archive pins in `input_archives`; observation and raw-audit files stay outside
the review packages. Preserve this metadata when passing the candidate on.
Missing held values must not be estimated automatically.

ERA climate handoffs require exact recognized archive pins. The current pin also
requires the complete ledger: `audit/date-holds.json` records its 900-row count
and SHA-256 fingerprint, derived from the checksum-verified published CSV. The
guard compares every field using fixed column order, sorted row keys and
length-prefixed UTF-8 text; missing values differ from empty strings. Row order
does not affect the fingerprint. Missing provenance, absent/empty/truncated
ledgers and altered findings fail closed. Historical replay is accepted only
with its explicit historical pins; absent metadata never selects that profile.

`audit/check_climate.R` rejects ERA Data.Out outcome rows with date holds,
even if a proposed downstream fill makes them look usable. Eight EO0115
outcomes have accepted planting dates but held harvest dates: they remain in
the 40,655 planting-date count and are blocked by the climate hold check. PD.Out,
Fert.Method and Res.Method holds remain linked to their own tables: they are
not joined to outcomes by study or guessed row correspondence. Any future
consumer of those dates must enforce the corresponding holds before inference.
Current climate checks use outcome planting/harvest anchors, not those tables.
Independent provider-grid checks, agreed seasonal rules and reviewed source
interpretation are still required; this correction does not certify SF-007.
